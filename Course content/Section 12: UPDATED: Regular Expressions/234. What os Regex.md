### MASTERING PATTERNS AND VALIDATIONS

#### What are Regular Expressions?

- **A sequeuce of characters that define a search pattern.**
These patterns are used to match and manipulate strings based on
specific criteria, allowing for powerful text processing and 
data vlidation.

#### Regular expression boundary
```makrdown
/[\w._%+-]+@[\w.-]+\.[a-ZA-Z]{2,4}/
```

- [\w._%+-] match anything contained within brackets
+           as many times as possible
@           matchthe @ symbol
[a-ZA-Z]    match upper and lower case A through Z

\w          match any character A-Z upper or lower
._%+-       match any ._%+-
.           match the . symbol
{2,4}       match at least two times but no more than four times

### Why Use Regular Expressions?

- **Validate inputs (emails, URLs, etc.).** Ensure that
user-entered data adheres to the required formate,
such as checking if an email address or URL is valid.

- **Search and replace text.** Efficiently find and replace
specific patterns within large texts, streamlining
data cleaning and modication tasks.

- **Extract specific patterns.** Identify and extract
meaningful data, such as phone numbers or dates,
from unstructed text for furher processing.
